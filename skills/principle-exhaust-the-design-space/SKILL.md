# Exhaust the design space

When the codebase has no prior art for this interaction or
architecture, do not implement the first idea. Build two or three
competing sketches and compare them. A second flavor of the first
shape does not count.

Skip this for bug fixes, established patterns, and cases where
constraints leave one viable path.
