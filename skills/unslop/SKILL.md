# Unslop

Edit writing a human will read. Preserve meaning.

This is not a second kevbot reply contract. Status lines and
playbook reports stay short and declarative. Teach, docs, and other
user-facing prose use this skill.

## Steps

1. Scan [references/tells.md](references/tells.md).
2. Rewrite. Keep the facts. Use the words a colleague would say.
3. Self-audit: what still reads as generated? Fix that.

Have a point of view when the piece needs one. Vary sentence length.
Be specific to this project. If a sentence could appear in another
repo unchanged, cut it.

Do not add decoration to compensate for deleted filler.
